Notes

- `Dicliptera tweediana` is treated here with `Dicliptera squarrosa` images because major taxonomic references list `Dicliptera tweediana` as a synonym of `Dicliptera squarrosa`.
- The `Flechilla` and `Cola de zorro` starter entries are genus-level entries, so their bundled photos are representative species images: `Nassella neesiana` and `Setaria parviflora`.
- The `Congorosa` entry uses images filed on Commons under both `Maytenus ilicifolia` and `Monteverdia ilicifolia`, which are the same species.
- Photos are resized to fit 860 by 1150 pixels and re-encoded as progressive JPEG so the whole set stays small enough to cache offline.
